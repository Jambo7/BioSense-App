Chapter 1
Purpose & Design Philosophy

1.1 Purpose
The purpose of this specification is to define the constitutional architecture governing how BioSense acquires, organises, validates, versions and maintains trusted scientific knowledge. Every constitutional engine depends upon scientific understanding. The Scientific Knowledge & Evidence Intelligence Engine serves as the authoritative source of that understanding, ensuring that every Health Belief, Decision Object, Prediction Object and Structured Explanation is ultimately grounded in governed scientific evidence.
Its responsibility is not to reason about individuals. Its responsibility is to preserve the scientific knowledge upon which constitutional reasoning depends.

Constitutional Principle
Reason about individuals using trusted science rather than reasoning about science itself.

1.2 Scope
This specification governs:
Scientific Knowledge Objects, 
Clinical Knowledge Objects, 
Evidence Objects, 
Guideline Objects, 
Mechanistic Knowledge, 
Evidence hierarchies, 
Scientific provenance, 
knowledge versioning, 
evidence lifecycle, 
knowledge validation, 
conflict resolution, 
scientific currency, 
retirement of obsolete knowledge, 
Canonical Scientific Library. 
It deliberately excludes:
individual Health Beliefs (ENG-001), 
observation processing (ENG-002), 
confidence evaluation (ENG-003), 
decision generation (ENG-004), 
communication (ENG-005), 
personalisation (ENG-006), 
forecasting (ENG-007). 
This engine owns scientific truth.
Other engines apply it.

1.3 Authority
The Scientific Knowledge Engine provides constitutional knowledge to every other engine.
Consumer engines may include:
ENG-001 
ENG-002 
ENG-003 
ENG-004 
ENG-005 
ENG-006 
ENG-007 
Consumer engines shall remain read-only.
Scientific knowledge ownership remains exclusively with ENG-008.

Engineering Rule
Scientific knowledge has one constitutional owner.
All other engines consume it.

1.4 Engineering Philosophy
Scientific knowledge evolves continuously.
New evidence emerges.
Guidelines change.
Consensus develops.
Previously accepted understanding may become obsolete.
BioSense shall therefore distinguish between:
permanent constitutional architecture, 
evolving scientific knowledge. 
The platform architecture should remain stable while scientific understanding evolves through governed updates.

Founder Principle
Allow science to evolve without redesigning the platform.

1.5 Constitutional Definition
For engineering purposes:
The Scientific Knowledge & Evidence Intelligence Engine is the constitutional system responsible for governing the acquisition, validation, versioning, organisation and lifecycle management of trusted scientific knowledge used throughout the BioSense Intelligence Platform.
It establishes scientific understanding.
It does not apply that understanding to individuals.

Constitutional Principle
Scientific knowledge should exist independently from individual health intelligence.

1.6 Knowledge Is Not Reasoning
A strict architectural boundary exists between scientific knowledge and constitutional reasoning.
Scientific knowledge answers:
"What is currently supported by trusted evidence?"
Reasoning answers:
"What does that mean for this individual?"
Those are separate constitutional responsibilities.
The Scientific Knowledge Engine shall never:
create Health Beliefs, 
interpret observations, 
calculate confidence, 
prioritise interventions, 
communicate recommendations. 
It supplies knowledge.
Other engines perform reasoning.

Engineering Rule
Knowledge informs reasoning.
It does not perform reasoning.

1.7 Scientific Knowledge Objects
The primary output of this engine shall be governed Scientific Knowledge Objects.
Illustrative examples include:
Biomarker Knowledge Object 
Disease Knowledge Object 
Physiological Mechanism Object 
Nutrition Knowledge Object 
Exercise Knowledge Object 
Sleep Knowledge Object 
Medication Knowledge Object 
Clinical Guideline Object 
Scientific Consensus Object 
Evidence Summary Object 
Each Scientific Knowledge Object shall possess:
provenance, 
evidence hierarchy, 
scientific confidence, 
publication history, 
supporting literature, 
version, 
review history, 
explainability, 
audit history. 

Founder Principle
Scientific understanding should exist as structured constitutional knowledge rather than scattered documents.

1.8 Explainability
Every Scientific Knowledge Object shall remain independently explainable.
BioSense shall always be capable of explaining:
what the scientific knowledge states, 
which evidence supports it, 
why BioSense accepts it, 
how current the knowledge is, 
which guidelines contributed, 
where uncertainty remains, 
when the knowledge was last reviewed. 
Scientific knowledge that cannot be explained shall not become constitutional knowledge.

Constitutional Principle
Scientific truth should remain transparent rather than assumed.

1.9 Scientific Boundaries
The Scientific Knowledge Engine shall never:
invent medical knowledge, 
speculate beyond available evidence, 
elevate weak evidence to established knowledge, 
conceal scientific disagreement, 
present hypotheses as consensus, 
replace expert medical literature. 
Scientific knowledge shall always remain proportionate to the quality of supporting evidence.

Founder Principle
The confidence of scientific knowledge should never exceed the confidence of the science itself.

1.10 Acceptance Criteria
This chapter shall be considered complete when:
✓ The Scientific Knowledge Engine is clearly separated from constitutional reasoning.
✓ Scientific Knowledge Objects are established as first-class constitutional objects.
✓ Scientific ownership boundaries remain unambiguous.
✓ Scientific knowledge evolves independently from platform architecture.
✓ Knowledge remains explainable, versioned and auditable.
✓ Scientific uncertainty is explicitly preserved.
✓ Every Scientific Knowledge Object remains constitutionally governed.
2.1 Purpose
The purpose of this chapter is to define the constitutional architecture governing how scientific knowledge is acquired, evaluated, validated and admitted into the Canonical Scientific Library.
Scientific literature evolves continuously.
However, not every publication represents constitutional scientific knowledge. The Scientific Knowledge & Evidence Intelligence Engine shall therefore operate a governed acquisition pipeline that transforms external scientific evidence into trusted Scientific Knowledge Objects. The objective is to ensure that every piece of knowledge consumed by BioSense has passed through a transparent, explainable and reproducible validation process.

Constitutional Principle
Scientific knowledge should be admitted through governance rather than ingestion alone.

2.2 Engineering Philosophy
Modern healthcare produces an enormous volume of research.
Not all research is:
equally rigorous, 
equally applicable, 
equally reproducible, 
equally relevant. 
The Scientific Knowledge Engine shall therefore distinguish between:
scientific publication, 
scientific evidence, 
constitutional knowledge. 
Only validated constitutional knowledge shall become available to downstream engines.

Founder Principle
Reading science is not the same as accepting science.

2.3 Scientific Sources
The acquisition architecture may consume knowledge from governed sources including:
peer-reviewed journals, 
systematic reviews, 
meta-analyses, 
recognised clinical guidelines, 
consensus statements, 
professional medical societies, 
regulatory publications, 
authoritative biomedical reference works. 
The platform shall preserve provenance for every acquired source.

Engineering Rule
Every scientific source shall possess an identifiable provenance before entering constitutional review.

2.4 Knowledge Acquisition Pipeline
Every Scientific Knowledge Object shall follow the same constitutional pipeline.
External Scientific Source

↓

Source Verification

↓

Evidence Classification

↓

Scientific Appraisal

↓

Knowledge Extraction

↓

Scientific Validation

↓

Scientific Knowledge Object

↓

Canonical Scientific Library

↓

Consumed by Constitutional Engines
No Scientific Knowledge Object shall bypass this process.

Constitutional Principle
Scientific knowledge should follow one governed constitutional pathway.

2.5 Source Verification
Before scientific evaluation begins, every source shall undergo verification.
Verification shall establish:
publication identity, 
publication status, 
publication date, 
source authenticity, 
publication integrity, 
citation traceability. 
Unverified sources shall not proceed further.

Founder Principle
Trust the source before trusting the science.

2.6 Evidence Classification
Verified sources shall be classified according to constitutional evidence categories.
Illustrative classifications include:
systematic review, 
meta-analysis, 
randomised controlled trial, 
observational study, 
cohort study, 
case-control study, 
case series, 
expert consensus, 
clinical guideline, 
mechanistic research. 
Classification does not determine acceptance.
It provides context for subsequent appraisal.

Engineering Rule
Evidence classification precedes evidence judgement.

2.7 Scientific Appraisal
Every classified source shall undergo structured scientific appraisal.
Illustrative appraisal dimensions include:
methodological quality, 
sample size, 
reproducibility, 
statistical robustness, 
external validity, 
population relevance, 
publication limitations, 
known biases, 
conflicting evidence. 
Scientific appraisal shall remain transparent and reproducible.

Constitutional Principle
Evaluate the quality of science before accepting its conclusions.

2.8 Knowledge Extraction
Following appraisal, the Scientific Knowledge Engine shall extract structured scientific understanding.
Illustrative examples include:
physiological mechanisms, 
biomarker interpretation, 
intervention evidence, 
risk relationships, 
guideline recommendations, 
evidence limitations, 
unresolved scientific questions. 
Knowledge extraction shall preserve scientific meaning without reproducing publications verbatim. Extracted knowledge shall remain linked to its supporting evidence.

Founder Principle
Extract understanding rather than copying literature.

2.9 Scientific Validation
Extracted knowledge shall undergo constitutional validation.
Validation shall confirm:
consistency with accepted scientific understanding, 
provenance integrity, 
evidence sufficiency, 
constitutional compatibility, 
explainability, 
reproducibility. 
Knowledge failing validation shall not become constitutional knowledge. Validation outcomes shall be permanently recorded.

Engineering Rule
Validation establishes constitutional acceptance rather than scientific certainty.

2.10 Canonical Scientific Library Admission
Only validated Scientific Knowledge Objects shall enter the Canonical Scientific Library.
Admission shall preserve:
Scientific Knowledge Object, 
supporting evidence, 
provenance, 
evidence hierarchy, 
version, 
review history, 
validation history, 
explainability metadata. 
Consumer engines shall access scientific knowledge exclusively through the Canonical Scientific Library.

Constitutional Principle
One canonical scientific library should serve the entire BioSense platform.

2.11 Engineering Constraints
The Scientific Knowledge Acquisition Framework shall:
require source verification, 
preserve provenance, 
separate classification from appraisal, 
require constitutional validation, 
prohibit direct publication ingestion, 
preserve explainability, 
remain fully auditable. 

2.12 Acceptance Criteria
This chapter shall be considered complete when:
✓ Every Scientific Knowledge Object follows the constitutional acquisition pipeline.
✓ Source verification precedes scientific appraisal.
✓ Evidence classification remains distinct from validation.
✓ Knowledge extraction preserves scientific meaning while maintaining traceability.
✓ Only validated Scientific Knowledge Objects enter the Canonical Scientific Library.
✓ Consumer engines access scientific knowledge exclusively through governed interfaces.
✓ Every stage of knowledge acquisition remains fully explainable and auditable.
Chapter 3
Scientific Knowledge Model & Canonical Scientific Knowledge Objects

3.1 Purpose
The purpose of this chapter is to define the canonical Scientific Knowledge Model used throughout the BioSense Intelligence Platform. Scientific knowledge shall exist as structured constitutional intelligence rather than collections of documents, publications or guidelines. Scientific Knowledge Objects provide the governed representation of accepted scientific understanding upon which all constitutional reasoning depends. They form the canonical interface between evolving medical science and the BioSense Intelligence Platform.

Constitutional Principle
Scientific understanding should exist as structured knowledge rather than unstructured literature.

3.2 Engineering Philosophy
Medical science is complex.
The same physiological concept may be supported by:
multiple publications, 
multiple guidelines, 
multiple systematic reviews, 
differing populations, 
evolving evidence. 
Rather than exposing this complexity directly to downstream engines, the Scientific Knowledge Engine shall synthesise accepted understanding into governed Scientific Knowledge Objects. Scientific Knowledge Objects become the constitutional source of scientific truth within BioSense.

Founder Principle
Engines should reason from scientific understanding rather than scientific documents.

3.3 Scientific Knowledge Domains
Every Scientific Knowledge Object shall belong to one or more Scientific Knowledge Domains.
Illustrative domains include:
Biomarker Knowledge
Scientific understanding of biomarker physiology, interpretation and significance.

Physiological Mechanisms
Mechanisms underlying biological processes.

Disease Knowledge
Current understanding of disease development, progression and prevention.

Nutrition Knowledge
Evidence relating to dietary patterns, nutrients and metabolic health.

Exercise Knowledge
Evidence relating to physical activity, recovery and performance.

Sleep Knowledge
Scientific understanding of sleep physiology and optimisation.

Medication Knowledge
General scientific understanding of medication mechanisms, interactions and evidence.

Longevity Knowledge
Evidence relating to healthy ageing and lifespan optimisation.

Clinical Guideline Knowledge
Structured representation of recognised clinical guidance.

Population Reference Knowledge
Scientific understanding of demographic and population-level variation. Each Scientific Knowledge Domain shall remain independently governed.

Engineering Rule
Scientific Knowledge Domains shall remain mutually distinguishable with clearly defined ownership.

3.4 Scientific Knowledge Objects
Each Scientific Knowledge Domain shall contain one or more Scientific Knowledge Objects.
Illustrative examples include:
ApoB Knowledge Object
Current scientific understanding of ApoB physiology, interpretation and evidence.

VO₂ Max Knowledge Object
Scientific understanding of aerobic fitness and associated health outcomes.

Sleep Consistency Knowledge Object
Evidence relating to sleep regularity and physiological recovery.

Omega-3 Knowledge Object
Scientific understanding of omega-3 physiology, evidence and limitations.

Biological Age Knowledge Object
Accepted scientific models relating to biological ageing.

Cardiometabolic Risk Knowledge Object
Current understanding of cardiometabolic disease mechanisms.

Exercise Recovery Knowledge Object
Evidence supporting recovery optimisation.

Constitutional Principle
Every scientific concept shall belong to one governed Scientific Knowledge Object.

3.5 Object Structure
Every Scientific Knowledge Object shall contain:
Object Identifier, 
Knowledge Domain, 
Scientific Statement, 
Supporting Evidence, 
Evidence Hierarchy, 
Scientific Consensus Status, 
Known Limitations, 
Population Applicability, 
Publication History, 
Version, 
Review History, 
Explainability Metadata, 
Consumer Engines. 
The constitutional structure shall remain consistent regardless of knowledge type.

Founder Principle
Every Scientific Knowledge Object should answer what is known, how well it is known and where uncertainty remains.

3.6 Relationships Between Scientific Knowledge Objects
Scientific Knowledge Objects may contribute to one another.
Illustrative examples include:

Implicit scientific dependencies shall be prohibited.

Engineering Rule
Scientific relationships shall remain transparent and independently explainable.

3.7 Knowledge Lifecycle
Every Scientific Knowledge Object shall progress through a governed lifecycle.
Illustrative stages include:

Constitutional Principle
Scientific understanding evolves through new versions rather than rewritten history.

Every Scientific Knowledge Object shall declare its current scientific status.
Illustrative states include:
Current Consensus.

Emerging Evidence.

Established Mechanism.

Limited Evidence.

Conflicting Evidence.

Superseded Knowledge.
Knowledge status shall evolve through constitutional governance. Scientific uncertainty shall remain explicitly represented.

Constitutional Principle
Scientific maturity should be represented as clearly as scientific content.

3.10 Explainability
Every Scientific Knowledge Object shall answer:
What scientific concept is represented? 
Which evidence supports this understanding? 
What is the current consensus status? 
Which populations does this knowledge apply to? 
What limitations exist? 
Which constitutional engines consume this knowledge? 
When was this understanding last reviewed? 

3.11 Engineering Constraints
The Scientific Knowledge Model shall:
remain domain-based, 
preserve constitutional ownership, 
support explicit scientific relationships, 
preserve knowledge currency, 
support historical reproducibility, 
remain explainable, 
remain fully auditable. 

3.12 Acceptance Criteria
This chapter shall be considered complete when:
✓ Scientific Knowledge Objects are established as the canonical representation of scientific understanding.
✓ Every scientific concept belongs to a governed Knowledge Domain.
✓ Scientific relationships remain explicit.
✓ Knowledge lifecycle stages are constitutionally defined.
✓ Scientific currency is explicitly represented.
✓ Consumer engine ownership remains unambiguous.
✓ Every Scientific Knowledge Object remains fully explainable and auditable.
Chapter 4
Evidence Hierarchies, Scientific Consensus & Conflict Resolution

4.1 Purpose
The purpose of this chapter is to define how the Scientific Knowledge & Evidence Intelligence Engine evaluates differing sources of scientific evidence, establishes constitutional scientific consensus and manages situations where scientific knowledge is incomplete, evolving or conflicting.
Medical science is dynamic.
Different studies, guidelines and expert groups may legitimately reach different conclusions. The Scientific Knowledge Engine shall therefore represent scientific agreement and disagreement explicitly rather than forcing artificial certainty. The objective is to ensure that BioSense reasons from the best available scientific understanding while preserving transparency regarding uncertainty and evolving evidence.

Constitutional Principle
Scientific disagreement should be represented rather than concealed.

4.2 Engineering Philosophy
Not all scientific evidence contributes equally to constitutional knowledge.
Some evidence is:
more rigorous, 
more reproducible, 
more generalisable, 
more widely accepted, 
more clinically applicable. 
Scientific consensus therefore emerges from the collective strength of evidence rather than from individual publications. The Scientific Knowledge Engine shall evaluate bodies of evidence rather than isolated studies whenever possible.

Founder Principle
Constitutional knowledge should emerge from scientific convergence rather than individual findings.

4.3 Constitutional Evidence Hierarchy
The Scientific Knowledge Engine shall evaluate evidence using a governed Evidence Hierarchy.
Illustrative hierarchy includes:
Level 1
Systematic reviews and high-quality meta-analyses.

Level 2
Major international clinical guidelines.

Level 3
Large randomised controlled trials.

Level 4
Well-designed cohort and observational studies.

Level 5
Mechanistic and physiological research.

Level 6
Expert consensus statements.

Level 7
Case reports and preliminary observations.
The Evidence Hierarchy supports appraisal.
It does not automatically determine constitutional acceptance.

Engineering Rule
Evidence hierarchy informs judgement without replacing scientific appraisal.

4.4 Scientific Consensus
Every Scientific Knowledge Object shall declare its current Consensus Status.
Illustrative states include:
Established Consensus.
↓
Strong Emerging Consensus.
↓
Moderate Consensus.
↓
Limited Consensus.
↓
Conflicting Evidence.
↓
Insufficient Evidence.
Consensus status reflects the current body of scientific understanding rather than individual publications. Consensus shall evolve only through constitutional governance.

Constitutional Principle
Consensus reflects the balance of evidence rather than unanimous agreement.

4.5 Scientific Conflict
Conflicting scientific evidence shall be represented explicitly.
Illustrative examples include:
Conflicting intervention studies.
↓
Conflicting guideline recommendations.
↓
Population-specific differences.
↓
Conflicting mechanistic explanations.
↓
Incomplete replication.
Conflict shall not prevent constitutional knowledge from existing.
Instead, the Scientific Knowledge Object shall explicitly describe:
the conflicting positions, 
supporting evidence, 
strength of competing evidence, 
current consensus position, 
remaining uncertainty.
